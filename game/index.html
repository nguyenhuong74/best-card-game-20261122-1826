FOUR WINDS
DUEL

An original duel of the four winds. Outplay the Warden of the Gale.

  • 30 HP each. Bring the Warden to 0 before yours runs out.
  • 3 energy a turn. Every card costs energy to play.
  • Resonance: play two cards of the same suit in one turn — the second gains +2.
  • Strikes deal damage, Wards block it, Mends heal, Insights draw cards.
  • Mouse or keyboard: 1–5 play a card, Enter ends the turn, R restarts.

A complete card game in your browser. No network, no account, no ads.
